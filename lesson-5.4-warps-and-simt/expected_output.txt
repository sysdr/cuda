Part 4: what the timing difference actually proves
  Verification: divergence_measurably_slower = PASS
  each thread still does exactly 200 iterations of real work either
  way -- the only difference is whether every thread in a warp took
  the same branch. when they don't, the warp runs BOTH branches,
  masking out whichever half isn't active for each one. that serial
  replay is the entire cost -- not extra math, just extra passes.

Part 5: why block sizes are usually multiples of 32
  a block size that isn't a multiple of the warp size leaves the
  last warp partially empty -- some lanes launched with nothing
  valid to do, still costing a full warp's worth of scheduling.
  this is a preview of occupancy tuning in module 10.
