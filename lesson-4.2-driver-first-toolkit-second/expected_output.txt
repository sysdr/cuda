=== What nvidia-smi's CUDA Version field actually means ===
  nvidia-smi's banner reports a 'CUDA Version' even with zero toolkit
  installed. That number describes driver capability, not an installed
  toolkit -- lesson 4.4 installs the actual toolkit and lesson 4.6's
  'nvcc --version' is what checks THAT number instead.

  nvidia-smi reports: CUDA Version: <!-- MEASURED: e.g. 13.3 -->
  this means: your driver can support CUDA Toolkit versions up to <!-- MEASURED -->
